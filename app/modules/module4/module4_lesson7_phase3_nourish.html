My wellness journey · Module 4 — Lesson 7

Phase 3 — Nourish (maintenance for life)

The last phase — but arguably the most important. This is not the finish line; it's the new starting point. The goal shifts from reversal to understanding your own body and maintaining metabolic health permanently.

Phase 3 at a glance

⏱Duration: indefinite. This is your lifestyle, not a phase to complete.
🕐Fasting: intuitive. Some days longer, some shorter. By this point it's a natural habit — no strict window required.
🍽Snacks: optional by choice — not by necessity. 3 fixed meals is still the default. If you snack, you already know what to choose.
🎯Objective: find your personal carb tolerance, deepen metabolic flexibility, eat intuitively — without weighing, counting, or logging. No food group is eliminated; everything is moderated.

The 80/20 rule — how to live in Phase 3

80% — eat as clean as possible
20% — enjoy life

The 80% is your daily baseline — real food, protein first, vegetables dominant, no naked carbs. The 20% is travel, celebrations, vacations, social occasions. This balance is permanent. You're not on a diet — you have a metabolic identity.

The most important Phase 3 rule — food order

In Phase 3 you're no longer restricted to specific combinations — but the order in which you eat still matters enormously. Eating in this sequence lowers your glucose response by 30–70%:

1 · Vegetables (fiber) → 2 · Protein → 3 · Fat → 4 · Carbs → 5 · Fruit
→Mixed plates: if protein and vegetables dominate, eating everything together is fine (e.g. tacos with meat = protein-dominant → okay together).
→Carb-dominant plates: even if there's protein in the dish (e.g. pasta with chicken), what dominates is carbs → eat a vegetable soup or salad first to create a fiber buffer.
→Use common sense — the reset principles are now part of your intuition, not a rigid system.

Ongoing monitoring — don't stop here

📊Run labs at least once a year (every 6 months if possible). Use the same markers from Module 3. Your body changes — especially with age, stress, and hormonal shifts.
📋Revisit the symptom questionnaire periodically. It's not just about symptoms — also reassess your goals. Where are you now? Where do you want to go?
📱Use your glucose monitor when in doubt — adding a new carb portion, testing a food, returning from vacation. The data always tells you the truth.

Having had IR makes you more prone to it returning. Ongoing awareness is not paranoia — it's the cost of keeping what you earned.

Phase fluidity — moving between phases is normal and expected

1
Reset
After vacations, illness, or losing connection with habits. A metabolic reboot — not a punishment.
1.5
Weekday reset
Phase 1 Mon–Fri, Phase 2 on weekends. Or alternate days freely.
2
Return to burn
If you feel better with tighter structure or if symptoms return. No shame — just data.
2.5
Weekday burn
Phase 2 Mon–Fri, Phase 3 on weekends. A sustainable middle ground for many.
3
Nourish
The permanent home. Return here after any temporary phase shift.

The goal is for everyone to eventually live in Phase 3. But the path is not linear — and moving between phases is the system working as designed, not a failure.

Phase 3 is yours to design. Take what worked in Phase 1, keep what worked in Phase 2, and layer in your own experiments. The reset gave you the tools — now use them your way.
