Phase 1 — Revert (deep dive)

The strictest phase — but also the most temporary. Two weeks of focused depletions to drop insulin, reduce inflammation, and begin fat burning. Think of it as a metabolic reboot, not a permanent diet.

Phase 1 at a glance

⏱Duration: minimum 2 weeks, maximum 4 weeks. Don't stay longer — the program is designed to progress.
🕐Fasting: start at 12 hours minimum. If already experienced with IF, go to 14–16 hrs.
🍽Meals: 3 fixed meals per day. No snacking. If hypoglycemia symptoms appear (shakiness, hunger + anger, energy crash), eat a protein + vegetable snack. Gradually eliminate snacks as metabolic flexibility builds.
🎯Goal: deplete glycogen, lower insulin, enter mild nutritional ketosis (fat burning begins). This is where the most visible results happen — reduced inflammation and weight loss.

What's eliminated in Phase 1

All gluten All dairy All grains (including corn, quinoa, rice) All alcohol
→Gluten hides in: sausages, deli meats, dressings, condiments, seasonings, beer. Read labels — the more processed, the more hidden gluten.
→Dairy: many people have unknown sensitivities. Eliminating for 2 weeks reveals this. Can be reintroduced in Phase 2.
→Grains: reducing them automatically reduces carbs. They can also be inflammatory to the gut.
→Carbs still allowed: starchy vegetables (sweet potato, beet, corn, plantain, jicama) and legumes (beans, lentils, edamame). These are high in fiber so net carbs are lower.

If a permitted carb (like sweet potato) spikes your glucose significantly, swap it for something that doesn't — or reduce the portion. Use your glucose data to personalize. The goal is what works for your body.

Phase 1 meal combination guide

Breakfast

Protein Vegetables Fruit (as dessert)

Keep it savory. Eat fruit last — as a dessert, not first. Start with eggs + vegetables, finish with berries.

Lunch

Protein Vegetables Fat Carb (1 portion)

Your one carb portion for the day goes here. Pair it with everything else to slow absorption.

Dinner

Protein Vegetables Fat

No carbs or fruit at dinner. Keep it simple and light. Most fat-burning happens overnight.

The ebook provides 8 menu ideas per combination. Mix and match freely — any breakfast option with any lunch option — as long as you stay within the correct meal category and portions. You don't have to follow it exactly; substitute any item with an equivalent from the food lists.

The "Keto Flu" — what to expect & how to manage it

Your body is switching fuel sources — from glucose to fat. This transition can cause temporary symptoms. Normal duration: 1–7 days. More common if you were previously eating a lot of carbs.

Headache Dizziness Nausea Insomnia Irritability Anxiety Brain fog

What to do:

Hydration + electrolytes

Most important fix. Drink 2L water/day + unsweetened electrolytes. Or: sparkling water + a pinch of Himalayan salt. The body loses minerals when switching to fat-burning.

Magnesium

400mg magnesium daily — increase to 800mg if symptoms are significant. Rapidly restores mineral balance and eases symptoms.

Eat more vegetables & protein

This is not a starvation diet. If hungry, eat more protein and vegetables. More vegetables = more minerals. Don't force restriction.

MCT oil

1 tbsp MCT oil (medium-chain triglycerides) = instant energy for the brain and body. Helps bridge the transition while fat-burning is developing.

Reduce caffeine

Caffeine can worsen symptoms during transition. Reduce or avoid until feeling better.

Lighter exercise

Scale back intensity while adapting. Walking, light yoga, or stretching instead of high-intensity work. Resume normal exercise once symptoms pass.

If symptoms persist beyond 7 days without improvement → move to Phase 2 and return to Phase 1 later. The program is designed to support you, not punish you.

When to stay in Phase 1 vs. move to Phase 2

↓Stay in Phase 1 longer if: fasting insulin is still above 10, nocturnal hypoglycemia persists, you have 5+ kg to lose and symptoms haven't improved.
→Move to Phase 2 early if: you get sick, you're pregnant, Phase 1 feels too demanding, or you don't need significant weight loss. You can always return.
✓Normal progression: after 2 weeks in Phase 1, advance to Phase 2. The design is intentional — Phase 2 is where you stay as long as needed to reach your goals.
