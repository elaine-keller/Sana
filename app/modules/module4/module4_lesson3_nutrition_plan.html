The Reset 5 nutrition plan — full detail

Not keto, not low-fat — the best of both. Sufficient protein, high fiber, moderate fat, controlled carbs, and timed eating. The equivalency system replaces calorie counting with a simple portion framework.

Which plan are you? — choose before starting

Plan A — under 70 kg (154 lbs)

Lower caloric needs. Fewer carb and fat portions. All other principles identical to Plan B.

Plan B — over 70 kg (154 lbs)

Higher caloric needs. Slightly more carb and fat portions allowed. Otherwise the same method.

Special modifications for pregnancy, breastfeeding, and high-performance athletes are in the ebook.

The golden rule across all phases: no carb or fruit ever goes alone. Always pair with protein, fat, or fiber. Naked carbs = glucose spike.

Food group rules

Protein

  • Animal protein at breakfast and lunch minimum
  • Best choices: salmon, fish, chicken, turkey, eggs
  • Legumes (beans, lentils, chickpeas) count as protein + carb — adjust portions accordingly
  • Choose lean cuts most of the time
  • Avoid processed meats: deli, salami, hot dogs, artificial protein products
  • Organic when possible

Carbohydrates — 4 rules

  • Eliminate sugar in all forms and anything made with flour (bread, pasta, cereal, pastries)
  • No liquid carbs — juice, sports drinks, sweetened coffees, sodas, honey, syrups
  • Limit to 1–2 carb servings per day, not spread throughout the day
  • Always dress your carbs — never eat them alone. Pair with protein, fat, or fiber
  • Best carb sources: starchy vegetables (sweet potato, beet, corn, jicama) and legumes — they come with fiber built in
  • Grains: eliminated in Phase 1. In Phase 2, max 2×/week at ½ cup

Fat — 4 rules

  • Eliminate all vegetable / seed oils (canola, corn, soy, sunflower). Switch to extra virgin olive oil
  • Best sources: avocado, olives, coconut, nuts, seeds, egg yolk, fatty fish, natural meat fat
  • Moderate portions — goal is to use stored body fat, not dietary fat, for fuel
  • Bonus: 85% dark chocolate counts as a fat portion and helps stabilize glucose

Fruit

  • Always choose whole fruit — never juice
  • Best choices: berries / red fruits (highest fiber, lowest sugar spike)
  • Always pair with protein or fat (e.g. apple + almonds, berries + Greek yogurt)
  • Low-sugar fruits preferred: strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, kiwi, green apple

Vegetables

  • 75% of your plate should be vegetables at every meal
  • More colors = more nutrients; more leafy greens = more fiber
  • Target: 30g of fiber per day from vegetables
  • Easy ways to add more: vegetable soups, spinach in smoothies, roasted vegetables
  • Non-starchy vegetables are unlimited in all phases

Time / meal frequency

  • 3 fixed meals per day — no snacking between meals
  • Snacks only if hypoglycemia is still an issue at the start
  • Every bite triggers insulin — even healthy snacks prevent fat burning
  • Fasting windows covered in detail in the fasting lesson
  • Plan A/B weight cutoff: 70 kg (154 lbs)
