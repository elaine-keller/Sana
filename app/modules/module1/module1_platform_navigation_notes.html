Sections of the portal

6 course modules core content
Main videos, organized top to bottom. Watch in order. Each video can be marked complete — this tracks your progress and unlocks a completion certificate at the end. Module 4 (the Reset Method) is the longest and is broken into sub-topics: nutrition, Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, menus, recipe book, drinks, etc.
Downloadable materials keep forever
All course downloads in one place: ebook (complete course), menus, recipe book, equivalencies guide, tracking sheets, and support resources. Download everything early — you only have 6 months of platform access, but the files are yours to keep.
Masterclasses deep dives
~2-hour standalone classes on the most-asked topics: cortisol, supplements, perimenopause/menopause, thyroid activity, and more. Each one is essentially its own mini-course — don't skip these.
Coach Rezeth new — AI
An AI assistant trained on all program content. Available 24/7 for specific questions about the Reset (phases, food choices, fasting, etc.). Best for content questions — not for personal medical consultations. May have occasional errors, so verify important details against the program materials.
Q&A bank
200+ questions organized by topic (meal plan, phases, supplements, etc.). Browse by category or search directly. Great alternative if you prefer not to use the AI chat.
Past Q&A recordings
Long recordings from previous cohorts. Not required — but useful if you want more detailed guidance or feel you need extra support. Pamela suggests starting with the most recent ones (January).
Community new
Think of it like a private Facebook group. Read and accept the rules on first entry. The team posts daily tips, fun facts, and motivation. Members share results, recipe photos, and form sub-groups (by city, life stage, etc.). Live session reminders also come through here.
Exclusive benefits
Discounts on recommended brands, products, and exercise platforms. Check periodically — the list grows over time.
