My wellness journey · Module 3 — Lesson 1

Metabolic health — what is it & how do you measure it?

Module 3 is about self-knowledge — learning to read your own body through three concrete tools. The goal isn't to become your own doctor, but to ask better questions and understand what the data means.

The 3 measurement tools
1
Symptom questionnaire
A structured self-assessment to identify patterns and severity of metabolic symptoms.
2
Lab work
Which tests to request, how to read and interpret results, and discounted lab packages.
3
Glucose monitor
Glucometer or continuous sensor — learning to read glucose graphs and understand food responses in real time.
